What to try

  1. Open the first screen. Wallet B is selected. Read the curve: grey is what happened, teal is the same trades with a rule, and the shaded part is trades the rule never saw.
  2. Switch the rule between halt after two losses and cap size after a loss. Watch the two numbers on the right: one is in-sample, one is held out.
  3. Pick Wallet A · control. The engine should leave this trader alone. Then pick Wallet F, the simulated trader built with a costly habit, to see what an accepted rule looks like.
  4. In Ask about this trader, ask one of the prompts on the right, then a follow-up. Open "Numbers locked" under an answer to see the facts behind every number.
  5. In Rulebook and Rule Gate, send a rule to the court, arm it, then check an order idea such as Buy $20k rNVDA. Nothing is ever placed.
  6. Come back here and run the selftest: fixed sentences, gold answers, timings, and the first run's misses.
