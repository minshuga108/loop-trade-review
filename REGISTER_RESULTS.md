# Review-lane head-to-head register (scaffolding), measured results

**Provenance.** The TradeMirror and tradememory columns are INDEPENDENT RE-IMPLEMENTATIONS OF THEIR PUBLISHED DEFINITIONS written in `engine/register.py`; no rival code was copied, downloaded or run, so a disagreement below may be our reading of their definition rather than their product. TradeMirror: README table 'The 4 heuristic rules' (github.com/hackid02/trademirror). tradememory: README example output (github.com/mnemox-ai/tradememory-protocol), which is descriptive and issues no verdict; the 'flag' used here is OUR reading convention (>= 5 trades after two losses and >= 25% of them at 1.5x usual size, anchored on its own example '5 of 20'). Every interpretation choice is written in the docstring next to each function. Loop columns run our engine unchanged.

Generated by `scripts/run_register.py`: 30 seeds per planted trader (seeds 500000-500029), 1000 permutations, runtime 72s. Planted traders are SIM_PLANTED; wallets are REAL_PLATFORM_PUBLIC (someone else's public Hyperliquid fills, never REAL_OWN).

## 5. Where we lose, plainly

- Loop has no detector for TradeMirror's weekend-rToken question or its exhaustion-cluster question: those columns are TradeMirror-only coverage. (Our overtrading detector lives in engine/detectors2.py and is not in this register yet.)
- Loop answers UNDERPOWERED far more often than the heuristics answer anything: on small or low-loss histories we say less.
- TradeMirror's weekend rule prices an ASSUMED 0.5% of notional; on the planted weekend trader the fills contain no extra cost, so any dollars it prices there are not in the data. That is a property of the published definition, measured here on our plant.
- The comparison is only as faithful as our reading of two README tables; see engine/register.py docstrings for every choice.
